# Art sources

Every piece of artwork Cart Catalog adds or changes, and how it was made. Any AI art here is only there because I
can't make it myself. If you would like to draw a replacement, I would love to use real art and will credit you
in full (see the end of this file).

Art the game draws unchanged from your own copy of Stardew Valley is not listed. Screenshots of the mod running
are not artwork and are not listed either.

## Made with AI

| File | What it is | How |
|---|---|---|
| [book.png](src/CartCatalog/assets/book.png) | The Cart Catalog book, the 16 x 16 furniture you place and open to order | a placeholder book sprite drawn as pixel art by AI (Claude, by Anthropic) for The Longest Year, recolored purple by an AI-written script, keeping the page strip cream |
| The title picture on the Nexus Mods page (not in this repository) | The "Cart Catalog" title card with crates | made with AI |

## Contributing art

Replacements from artists are welcome and get full credit here, in the README and in the release notes. For the
book, a 16 x 16 PNG with the same file name is all it takes. Open an issue or a pull request on GitHub, or leave
a comment on the Nexus Mods page.
